About Us

A Recipe Born
from Persistence

In 1952, Harland “Colonel” Sanders perfected his pressure-fried chicken and began serving it at his gas station in Corbin, Kentucky. Travelling the country in his white suit and black tie, he eventually franchised the concept — and KFC became a global phenomenon.

Today, our cooks still follow the same golden rules: fresh chicken, 11 herbs and spices, pressure-fried for exactly the right amount of time. Some traditions are worth keeping.

  • Pressure-fried for maximum crisp
  • 11 herbs & spices, secret since 1940
  • Chicken raised with care
  • Cooked fresh to order, every time
